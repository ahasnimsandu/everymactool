# EveryMacTool 1.0.4

- Fixed Performance mode slowing live power sampling to four seconds. Power readings now keep the selected interval in both modes.
- Refresh immediately on wake, app activation, and when the menu panel opens, with short retries while macOS resumes sensor reporting.
- Separate fast power sampling from slower device scans and chart history work.
- Show the age of the latest sample in the panel header.
- Apple Intelligence remains an optional local summary writer and does not gate or delay live measurements.

The app polls power telemetry every 0.25 seconds by default. macOS can publish sensor values less frequently than the app polls.
