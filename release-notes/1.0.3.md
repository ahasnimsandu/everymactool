## Faster live power and smoother trends

- Poll the macOS power controller every 0.25 seconds and animate menu-bar watt changes. macOS may publish new hardware telemetry at a slower rate; repeated polls do not fabricate new measurements.
- Replace the blocky energy chart with a smooth, 90-second trend sampled twice per second. Adapter input, Mac system load, and battery transfer are distinct series.
- Add a sustained high-battery-use alert when the Mac is on battery and measured load stays above 10 W.
- Identify the Apple silicon chip and performance/efficiency CPU-core layout, alongside measured whole-Mac system draw. macOS does not publish separate CPU, GPU, or Neural Engine watt readings to regular apps.

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. The app is ad-hoc signed; first launch may require approval in System Settings.
